# Recruit AI VC deck — founder inputs before sending

The presentation is fully editable. Yellow fields are intentionally left as placeholders because the repository does not contain verified investor metrics.

Replace these items before sharing with investors:

- Slide 7: verified target-agency count, validated annual contract value, and the resulting initial serviceable market.
- Slide 9: pilot price, team ACV, measured gross margin, and CAC payback.
- Slide 11: customers or pilots, revenue, product usage, and retention or repeat-role rate.
- Slide 12: founder name and relevant proof, round size, runway, and target close date.

Useful proof to add when available:

- One permissioned customer quote.
- One measured pilot case study: batch size, review time, shortlist acceptance, and measurement period.
- Cost per completed screening batch, including retries.
- The specific use of funds tied to 12-month milestones.

Do not add unsupported claims such as customer counts, SOC 2 status, bias elimination, guaranteed speed, or hiring accuracy.
